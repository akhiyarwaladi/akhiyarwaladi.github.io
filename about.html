About

Biography

I am Akhiyar Waladi, a lecturer (Asisten Ahli) in the Department of Electrical Engineering, Faculty of Science and Technology, Universitas Jambi, where I teach and research topics in artificial intelligence, machine learning, pattern recognition, and predictive systems.

My path into computing started at IPB University (Institut Pertanian Bogor), where I earned my Bachelor of Computer Science in 2017. During my undergraduate years I explored embedded systems and computer vision, co-developing an Automated Fish Counter, an embedded system integrated with an Android device for automated fish measurement, counting, and sorting (showcased on Devpost). I continued to a Master of Computer Science at Universitas Indonesia (2018-2020), where I deepened my work on pattern recognition and machine learning, including research on automatic counting of scanned election forms using feature matching and convolutional neural networks.

Between 2018 and 2024 I worked in industry as a Data Scientist, first at kumparan (2018-2020) in Jakarta and then at Alfagift, Global Loyalty Indonesia (2020-2024) in Tangerang. In these roles I built and maintained data products, forecasting systems, recommendation and segmentation models, and end-to-end machine-learning pipelines used by millions of retail customers.

Since September 2024, I have been a lecturer at Universitas Jambi. I currently teach undergraduate courses in the electrical engineering program, supervise student projects, and lead community-service (pengabdian) programs that bring AI and IoT literacy to schools, pesantren, and vocational students in the Jambi region.
