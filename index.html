I work on computer vision and predictive systems, from satellite images and microscope slides to forecasts for real use. This site collects my research, course materials, and projects in one place.

Open Bahan Ajar View Research About Me

I am a lecturer (Asisten Ahli) in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Universitas Jambi. My work centers on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Pattern Recognition, with a focus on making them useful outside the lab, whether for agriculture, health, or industry.

Before joining Universitas Jambi in 2024, I worked as a Data Scientist at Alfagift (Global Loyalty Indonesia) and kumparan, where I built forecasting systems, segmentation models, and ML pipelines used by millions of people.

I hold an M.Kom. from Universitas Indonesia and an S.Komp. from IPB University. My papers appear in Scopus-indexed and SINTA-accredited venues. See Google Scholar and SINTA.

Selected Research

Research

My research follows problems that students and collaborators bring in. Four strands:

Pattern Recognition

Reading scanned forms, counting fish from images, classifying satellite scenes and spotting malaria parasites.

Vision · CNN · YOLO

Predictive Systems

Forecasting with SARIMA and LSTM on energy exports and stock prices, and KNN-based trend analysis.

Time series · SARIMA · LSTM

Clustering and Segmentation

K-means and hierarchical methods for customer groups, province profiles and fire hotspots.

K-means · Hierarchical

Intelligent Systems

Small decision tools built for real users, from village IoT monitors to admin systems for pesantren.

IoT · Decision support
